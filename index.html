About me

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Sorbonne University, where I worked in the Operations Research group at LIP6 under the supervision of Spyros Angelopoulos and Christoph Dürr.

Before moving to Paris, I completed my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Applied Mathematics and Informatics at Novosibirsk State University under the supervision of Adil Erzin. I’m grateful for his guidance, which helped shape my interest in algorithms and optimization.

I enjoy testing assumptions, turning ideas into code, and explaining results clearly.
